  • Sync Zerodha pulls today's executed trades (Zerodha only shares the current day), so click it after the close each day. Buys add to an open position as pyramids, sells fill the exits, a new stock opens a new trade. Clicking twice never double counts. Zerodha asks you to log in once a day.
  • Import broker file reads a tradebook CSV (Zerodha: Console, Reports, Tradebook). Buys and sells are paired per stock into trades with up to three entries and three exits. Importing again updates the same trades and keeps your setup, stop, plan and notes. Include the date range from when you opened any position still held.
  • Avg entry blends the first buy and up to two pyramids. R is total profit or loss divided by the initial risk (entry minus stop, times total quantity).
  • PF impact is realised profit as a share of capital; Cum PF adds it up in date order. Risk now is what the open quantity loses at the trailing stop (or the stop), as a share of capital.
  • Win rate counts fully closed trades. Drawdown is the fall from the peak of capital plus realised profit.
