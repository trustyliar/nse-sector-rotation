  • Patterns: flag after a 30%+ move; tight 5-day range; inside day; narrowest range in 7 days; at the 10, 20 or 50 EMA; turning up off the EMA (closes above the prior day's high after touching the 20 EMA); episodic pivot (6%+ gap on 3x volume in the last 5 sessions, still holding); breakout today (50-day closing high on 1.5x volume).
