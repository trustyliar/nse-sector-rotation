Building = base still forming, or overhead supply: a red candle of 8%+ in the last 25 sessions that was followed by more selling (12%+ over three sessions) and that price has not closed back above; also still deep (20%+ below the high, needs time) and wide red candles (three or more red days of 7%+ since the high). All three lose 30% of their quality score. Stocks in the 5% circuit band are left out entirely. Trigger is today's high, the buy-stop level for tomorrow; Stop is today's low, kept between 0.4 and 1.5 daily ranges; Risk is the gap between them.
  • Quality (0-100): tightness 25%, prior run 20%, EMA alignment and closeness 20%, time in a base 15%, shallow pullback 10%, volume dry-up 10%. Stocks still making new highs with no base, and stocks with a daily range under 3%, are marked down. Grade A+ is 75 or more, A is 65+, B is 55+.
  • Rank score: 65% quality, 20% relative strength, 15% theme strength, so a clean setup in a leading theme ranks highest.
  • Prior run: the sharpest move into the 60-session high, measured from the low of the 30 sessions before it. Off high: distance below the 60-session high. Base: sessions since that high. Base vol / run vol: average volume in the base against the run before it. ADR: average daily range over 20 sessions.
  • Patterns: flag after a 30%+ move; tight 5-day range; inside day; narrowest range in 7 days; at the 10, 20 or 50 EMA; turning up off the EMA (closes above the prior day's high after touching the 20 EMA); episodic pivot (6%+ gap on 3x volume in the last 5 sessions, still holding); breakout today (50-day closing high on 1.5x volume).
